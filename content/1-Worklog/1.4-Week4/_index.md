### Week 4 Objectives:

* Understand virtual servers and Amazon EC2.
* Learn the main components required to launch an EC2 instance.
* Understand basic access, storage, security, and pricing concepts for EC2.

### Week 4 Achievements:

* Understood the purpose and core components of Amazon EC2.
* Selected basic instance types according to workload requirements.
* Understood AMIs and the EC2 instance lifecycle.
* Explained how Security Groups and key pairs protect instance access.
* Understood the relationship between EC2, EBS, and snapshots.
